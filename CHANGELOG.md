# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-18

- Collapse saved groups by default and keep recently visited pages expanded.
- Open individual saved tabs directly from a group card.
- Clear saved groups or browsing history in one action.
- Remove duplicate recently visited records while keeping the newest entry.

## 0.1.3 — 2026-09-03

- Move the page tagline into the header and remove the oversized hero section.
- Bring search and saved tab groups higher into the initial viewport.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-09-03

- Hide single-tab snapshots from the recovery section.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-09-03

- Capture recently closed multi-tab windows shown in Chrome's history menu.
- Restore a live Chrome session when available, with saved URLs as a fallback.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-02

- Override New Tab with a searchable card-based browser history.
- Automatically retain recent tab-group snapshots across restarts.
- Restore a saved group with its title, color, tab order, and URLs.
- Keep all data local with no host permissions or network requests.
