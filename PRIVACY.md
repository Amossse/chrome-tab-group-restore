# Privacy Policy

Groupback reads browser history and tab-group metadata only to render the New Tab dashboard and restore groups requested by the user.

- Data is stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on the user's device.
- The extension makes no network requests.
- There are no accounts, analytics, ads, trackers, or remote code.
- Incognito tabs and browser-internal URLs are not collected.
- Removing the extension removes its saved group snapshots. Browser history remains managed by the browser.

Last updated: September 2, 2026.
